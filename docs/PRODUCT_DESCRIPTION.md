# Cooked — Product Description

**Cook more of what you already have.**

Cooked is the pantry-first cooking app that turns the ingredients sitting in your
kitchen into meals you'll actually make. Instead of starting with a recipe and a
shopping trip, Cooked starts with what's on your shelves — then helps you plan,
shop, cook, and keep track of it all in one place.

---

## Why people love Cooked

- **Waste less food.** Know exactly what you have and what's about to expire.
- **Decide faster.** Stop staring into the fridge — see what you can cook right now.
- **Eat better.** Built-in calorie info on thousands of ingredients keeps you informed.
- **Stay organized.** Your pantry, shopping list, meal plan, and cooking history,
  all working together.

---

## What you can do

### 🍳 Discover recipes worth making
Browse a rich catalog of recipes — from quick weeknight meals to community
favorites and classic collections like Filipino and Japanese dishes. Search and
filter by cuisine and mood to find exactly what you're in the mood for.

### ✍️ Build your own recipe collection
Add your own recipes with ingredients, step-by-step instructions, and tags. Edit
them anytime. Your kitchen, your recipes.

### ⭐ Trust real ratings
See community star ratings — and add your own. Because you can only rate a recipe
after you've actually cooked it, every rating comes from real experience.

### ❤️ Save your favorites
Bookmark the recipes you love and find them again in a tap.

### 🥫 Track your pantry
Keep a live list of what's in your kitchen — quantities, and even expiration dates.
This is the heart of Cooked: it's what makes everything else smarter.

### 📋 Reuse your staples
Save go-to ingredient lists as templates ("Weekly staples," "Baking basics") and
restock your pantry in seconds.

### 🔍 Tap into a huge ingredient library
Nearly 7,000 ingredients with calorie information, ready to use — and you can add
your own or fine-tune any entry to match how you really cook.

### 📅 Plan your week
Map meals onto a calendar across breakfast, lunch, snack, and dinner. Plan as
simply or as thoroughly as you like.

### 🛒 Shop smarter
Build a shopping list of what you actually need. Add the same item twice and Cooked
combines it automatically — no messy duplicates.

### 👨‍🍳 Cook with confidence
When you cook a recipe, Cooked automatically updates your pantry (using up the
oldest stock first) and tallies the calories for what you made. No manual
bookkeeping.

### 📖 Look back on what you've cooked
Your cooking history keeps a record of every meal you've made and what went into
it.

### ⚙️ Make it yours
Manage your profile, preferences, and how information like calories is displayed.
