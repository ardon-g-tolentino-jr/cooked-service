# Cooked — Features

Cooked is a pantry-first recipe app: it helps you keep track of the ingredients
you actually have at home, find recipes you can make with them, plan your week,
and shop for what's missing.

This page describes everything the app can do, in plain language. No technical
knowledge needed.

---

## Getting started

### Create an account
Sign up with your name, email, and a registration code. You don't pick a password
during sign-up — instead, a temporary password is emailed to you. Sign in with it,
and the app will ask you to set your own password the first time.

### Sign in
Log in with your email and password.

### Sign in with Google
Prefer not to manage a password? Use the **Sign in with Google** button to log in
with your Google account instead.

### Forgot your password
Request a reset and a new temporary password is emailed to you. After signing in
with it, you'll be prompted to choose a new password. (For security, the app gives
the same confirmation message whether or not the email is registered.)

### Trial accounts
If you join on a trial, you get **full, unlimited access for your first 14 days**.
After the trial window ends, some features become limited (for example, a cap on
how many recipes you can create, and certain tools may be turned off). Your trial
window is fixed when you sign up.

## Recipes

### Browse and search recipes
Explore a built-in catalog of recipes, including community favorites and classic
collections (such as Filipino and Japanese dishes). Search and filter to find
something to cook.

### Recipe details
Open any recipe to see its full ingredient list, step-by-step instructions, the
cuisine it belongs to, and its mood tags (e.g. "comfort", "quick", "healthy").

### Create your own recipes
Add your own recipes with ingredients, instructions, cuisine, and mood tags.

### Edit recipes
Update a recipe's details, ingredients, instructions, or mood tags at any time.

### Rate recipes
Give community recipes a 1–5 star rating. You can only rate a recipe **after
you've cooked it at least once**, so ratings reflect real experience. Each recipe
shows its average rating and how many people have rated it.

### Save favorites
Bookmark recipes you love so you can find them again quickly in your saved list.

## Your kitchen

### Pantry
Keep a running list of the ingredients you currently have at home, including how
much of each and (optionally) when they expire. This is the heart of the app —
it's what lets Cooked tell you what you can cook right now.

### Pantry templates
Save a named list of ingredients (for example, "Baking basics" or "Weekly
staples") so you can quickly stock your pantry again later without re-typing
everything.

### Ingredient catalog
The app comes with a large built-in catalog of nearly 7,000 ingredients, each with
calorie information (sourced from the USDA food database). You can also:
- **Add your own** custom ingredients, and
- **Adjust** an ingredient's details (name, category, calories, weight per piece)
  to match what you actually use.

Your custom ingredients stay private to you.

## Planning and shopping

### Weekly meal planner
Plan your meals on a calendar. Assign recipes to specific days and meal slots —
breakfast, lunch, snack, or dinner — and add as many recipes per slot as you like.

### Shopping list
Build a shopping list of ingredients you need to buy. Adding the same ingredient
twice combines the amounts instead of creating duplicates. Remove items
individually or clear the whole list at once.

---

## Cooking and history

### Cook a recipe
When you cook a recipe, Cooked records it and **automatically deducts the
ingredients you used from your pantry** (using the oldest stock first). It also
calculates the calories for what you made, respecting any ingredient adjustments
you've set.

### Cook history
Review everything you've cooked over time, including a snapshot of the ingredients
and amounts used for each cook.

---

## Your profile

### Profile and account
View and update your display name and account details.

### Change your password
Update your password whenever you like from your account settings.

### Preferences and settings
Adjust your personal settings, such as how calories are displayed.
